## 1. The finite-state constant is an acyclic monotone composition

Keep

\[
 h={1\over200},\quad \kappa={1\over100000},\quad
 \delta={1\over2}-3\kappa,
 \quad B_j={3\over2}-2\kappa+j\delta,
 \quad \Lambda={3\over2}+2h.                         \tag{1}
\]

For fixed finite cycle `j`, Cartesian jet `m` and requested tail power `N`,
the stage-indexed compact graph selects finite derivative ceilings, finite
inverse-distance poles and a finite harmonic set. Let `R=R_(j,m,N)` be their
maximum. On the unchanged compact support hull collect:

\[
 \mathfrak P_R=\max\{\hbox{all selected profile, root, frame, source,
 pressure and mean weighted seminorms through }R\},    \tag{2}
\]

\[
 g_R=\min\{\hbox{all selected Jacobian, normal, root, determinant,
 variance and support gaps through }R\},              \tag{3}
\]

and the finite compact/slow inverse operator archive `K_R`. Every graph edge
is a finite sum, product, derivative, composition, reciprocal with denominator
at least `g_R`, constrained ODE, pressure/moment map, or compact/slow inverse.
Consequently there is a finite monotone composition

\[
 C_{j,m,N}
 =F_{j,m,N}(1+\mathfrak P_R,1+g_R^{-1},1+K_R)<\infty. \tag{4}
\]

Here `F` is obtained by composing the finitely many tame product,
composition, reciprocal, ODE and archived inverse estimates. It need not be a
literal polynomial in an inverse-operator norm. Both `F` and `R` may be
enormous and depend arbitrarily on `j,m,N`; they are finite because the graph
is finite and acyclic. No uniformity in the cycle or derivative order,
convergence of an uncut series, or numerical materialization is used.

The graph retains all six covariance entries, radial and axial velocity,

## 2. Regular and tail rows combine at one finite stage

\[

\]

\[

\]

derivatives remain inside the regular operator, along with pressure gradients,

\[
