## 1. Finalized leading profile: fixed order before any supremum

On the activation collar, the flat Volterra quotient has the exact integral

\[
 {\cal V}_m[b](s)={1\over2g(s)}\int_0^\infty e^{-v}
 (1+s^2v)^{-(m+3)/2}

 b\!\left({s\over\sqrt{1+s^2v}}\right)\,dv.       \tag{1}
\]

For every fixed number of `s` derivatives, the differentiated integrand is
bounded by `e^-v` times a polynomial in `v`; every such majorant is
integrable. Fixed angular derivatives act on the inherited analytic core
factor. Hence dominated differentiation supplies every fixed mixed jet
through the flat endpoint. The strip and constants may depend on the order.

The continuation, selected finite-frequency modulation, exact five-row
repair, annular roots and exterior heat map occupy finitely many closed

The heat endpoint is only smooth with one-sided jets; the provider explicitly
rejects a false common complex strip there. Thus the leading-profile theorem

\[
 \forall r<\infty\quad \exists C_r<\infty,          \tag{2}
\]

and does not assert one constant or strip uniform in `r`.

## 2. Background selector: the finite-head/geometric-tail split

At coefficient order `n`, the Volterra word expansion has at most
`ceil(k/2)` surviving Cauchy losses. The radial simplex factor

\[
 {z^{k+1}\over(k+1)!}

\]

coefficient has every fixed jet before its scale `a_n` is chosen. The real
selector then includes rows `0<=m<=n` and gives

\[
 C_{n,m}q^{nh}<2^{-n},\qquad m\le n,quad q\le a_n^{-1}. \tag{4}
\]

For a later fixed request `m`, (4) is enough even though the selector at an
early coefficient did not anticipate it. Split the positive coefficient
orders into

\[
 1\le n<m\quad\hbox{and}\quad n\ge\max\{1,m\}.       \tag{5}
\]

The first set has exactly `max(m-1,0)` elements, and each term has a finite
`m`-jet by (3). On the second set, `m<=n`, so (4) gives the geometric tail.
This proves every fixed Borel jet, including `m=R_*`, without changing any
previously selected scale and without looping through `R_*` indices in the
proof.
