# Q61: remove high Borel jets from the principal axial moment

A comparison **inside the estimate**, not a replacement physical wave,
allows the full-minus-leading F,G corrections to enter Q57 at low order.
Fix output n and put m=n+5. Assume the original accepted-band value
floors (including the tangential entrance and normalized column), ambient
propagator, chart/support and Q56–58 lower-order field bounds. All
polynomials below have nonnegative coefficients in S and fixed-order
constants. The finite-head estimate supplies the full-minus-leading F,G
difference through m+1 at cost ε² times a polynomial; no smallness of
high derivatives of these differences is assumed.

## Compare on the same paths

For each frozen label use superscript 0 for the formulas for n,n',K,
entrance and ambient generator evaluated with F⁰,G⁰, the leading fields
of Q58. Keep the actual carriers, k, ε, shear/growth reference parameters,
P, length, frame constants c0, tilt, cutoffs, covering and slow target.
In particular do not round a second phase or solve a new cone problem.
We compare only the principal *tensor coefficient after angular
averaging*, where the phase cancels exactly. This gives no estimate
for the pointwise difference of the oscillating physical waves.

The phase formulas and Q60's affine normal give n−n⁰,motion−motion⁰,K−K⁰
of order ε² through m in fixed-path coordinates, with polynomial costs
(v≤L≤S). Choose upper polynomials D,Q≥1 for |n−n⁰|/ε² and the inverse
length of the true tangential normal, respectively. If ε² DQ≤1/2,
both reference normal lengths (full and tangential) have reciprocals
at most 2Q, throughout the path. This uses a vector floor, never a
component floor. For reciprocals use f⁻¹−g⁻¹=(g−f)/(fg);
for inverse lengths also use the sum of the two positive lengths in
the difference of square roots. Leibniz and differentiated reciprocals
give, through m, polynomial bounds and ε² difference bounds for
the normalized entrance b and the complete ambient generator Ahat
of the canonical entrance lemma. These operations retain -n motionᵀ/N,
the axial component of motion, actual damping and the Gaussian rate.
P is common and is independent of x at fixed v and label. Bound
||Ahat⁰−Ahat||≤ε² E; let K≥1 be the true ambient two-time constant.

In the norm sup |t|/P on each subinterval the Volterra operator
with kernel Vhat(v,w)(Ahat⁰−Ahat)(w) has norm ≤ε² L K E.
If this is ≤1/2, the reference propagator satisfies
||Vhat⁰(v,w)||≤2K P(v)/P(w), by summing the Neumann series.
No exponential of a polynomial in S, or inverse minimum of P,
is needed. Differentiating the reference equation at fixed v now
bounds its pure-slow jets divided by P by polynomials, using only
leading jets and this value propagator. For the difference u=t⁰−t use

    u' = Ahat u + (Ahat⁰−Ahat)t⁰,     u(0)=P(0)(b⁰−b).

Slow derivatives of u through m cost ε² P times polynomials:
apply the true propagator, inducting on order, with differentiated
Ahat times lower u and the full product rule on the forcing. Positive
path derivatives follow by differentiating this same identity, with
one fewer path slot each time. Thus all mixed difference jets through
m retain ε²; derivatives of the varying reference rate in Ahat are
included. The identical Q60 affine cutoff maps transfer these bounds
to the localized h, including all cutoff derivatives. The higher
reference jets needed by Q60 itself use only F⁰,G⁰ through n+99998.

## Compare the squared coefficient, without a reference positive root

define z⁰=(Hbar⁰)⁻¹ Tβ, z=Hbar⁻¹ Tβ. Exactly,

    z⁰−z = (Hbar⁰)⁻¹ (Hbar−Hbar⁰) z.

Derivatives through m retain the difference rate by the differentiated
matrix equations. No positivity is required of z⁰, since it is never
square-rooted. Use Q59's formula ηβ² zσ hσ,a hσ,b/s₀, with the
same slow partition for both tensors. Raw jets of the difference
through m are bounded by ε² times a polynomial, including 1/s₀.

## Pay the low-order difference before estimating the shifted integral

Use the four leading weights of Q58 (its full-weight error is already
accounted for). In Q57's direct whole-line inequality the principal
tensor has prefactor ε_*², one Z derivative and at most four additional
derivatives for the shear inverse. Thus the tensor difference costs
O₄ at output n, including the slow weight derivative, the pressure
cutoff moment and its derivative. The O_(3−2κ) curl and mean remainder
is untouched. Only the comparison tensor goes through Q57's two high
integration counts. Its unstripped target and partition are the
original ones; its high column, entrance and path estimates are
Q59–60 with the two value constants doubled as above. The high
background norms in this calculation now come solely from the finalized
leading profile (including modulation, repair and pressure memory).
For the unstripped target a direct finite Taylor composition of its
smooth closed-shell jets with the chart of the target lemma suffices.
The slow partition is bounded by that lemma's dyadic/mesh convolutions
at width S⁻³. Neither is differentiated in the auxiliary direction.

These smallness tests need not redefine the accepted bands. For a common
frozen neighborhood take B(S*)≥1 dominating DQ, LKE and K₀C
for all its labels, with S_i≤25S*/16; build E,C using the preceding
conditional bounds, so B involves no unknown reference inverse or
reference propagator. If ε_*² B≤1/2 all comparisons hold on the
whole hull. Otherwise apply the original direct bound to the **true**
principal tensor only, giving ε_*² times a polynomial from jets through
m. For g=1−5κ in (0,1), on this second case ε_*^(-g)≤2B
(since ε_*≤1). Hence even this direct bound is O_(3−5κ) with
a larger polynomial. This case split uses only frozen scale, outside
all derivatives; no cutoff or derivative-dependent physical selector
is introduced.
