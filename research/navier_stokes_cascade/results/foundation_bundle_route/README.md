# Foundation-bundle route closure and the independent-validation frontier

Parent: `e140b4348b0a3ad3bcb26a8b2f26c79b2c31a8e6`.
Status: `foundation-route-closed-with-independent-continuum-validation-frontier`.

The parent joined the explicit later tail to the canonical covariance,
generated-source, complete finite-residual, realization, localization and
fresh-force maps.  It left a single broad phrase as the remaining obstruction:
the retained core/background and defining-map foundation bundle.  This
checkpoint audits that phrase.

There are two different questions:

1. **Route closure.**  Does every named premise have an earlier analytical
   provider, and can those providers be ordered without using a downstream
   conclusion to prove an upstream input?
2. **Independent validation.**  Have the continuum arguments in all of those
   providers been independently reproved, formally certified or externally
   reviewed on the same selected maps?

The answer to the first question is yes and to the second is no.  The new
result is the acyclic provider join below.  It corrects the vague implication
that one unidentified inequality still blocks the later-tail construction.
It does **not** relabel routed records or passing tests as an independent
continuum proof, and it does not prove an unconditional Navier--Stokes pulse,
residual, force or blow-up theorem.

Historical fields, evidence, roots, gauges, width `C^-20`, schedules and
forces are unchanged.  In particular the explicit later-tail field and force
remain distinct from every uncertified historical first-band object.

Its stable topological sort visits every node, and every edge points forward.
Adding the edge from the final residual/force node back to the core is rejected
as a cycle.  This is a dependency result, not a new estimate inside any node.

Three apparent loops are not mathematical cycles:

* The exact core takes a pressure-family datum, while
  `core_pressure_identification` later identifies that already defined datum

  reference before the radial scale and the global edits are chosen; the

* `matching_root_foundation` uses unmodulated compact input estimates to
  identify the accepted repair root.  Those estimates precede the repair;
  the repaired cone is a downstream consequence in `regional_cone_join`.
* The signed/mean target at a finite correction stage is recomputed from the
  current state.  `current_target_cycle_bridge` proves a forward recurrence;
  it does not assume a target produced by the later residual step.

Therefore no downstream conditional comparison theorem is needed to define
or accept an upstream profile/root in the documented route.

## 2. The relevant inequalities and their providers

The route keeps the actual quantitative gates instead of replacing them by
the word “finite.”  At the main interfaces they are:

1. The core map has displacement `F_b/Lambda` and Lipschitz factor
   `L_b/Lambda`, both strictly below the selected contraction bounds.  The
   recovered fixed point has positive real `Phi`; the core-growth theorem
   supplies the missing positive shear/growth alternative and `K_act<C^3`.
2. For an accepted five-row root, the pointwise Jacobian satisfies

   \[
   \|B^{-1}(J_c-B)\|\le 2\beta q\|c\|<\tfrac12,
   \qquad \|J_c^{-1}\|\le2\beta .                 \tag{1}
   \]

   The selected compact frequency also retains
   `4 beta^2 q D/N < 1/2`.  Higher fixed jets come from differentiating the
   same root equation, not from choosing a new root in each norm.
3. The regional provider proves the leading value gaps on the entire active
   compact region, including the corrected first-outer-ramp floor.  The
   exterior provider supplies the exact heat attachment and flat-edge jets.
4. The complete five-row identities give

   \[
   M_\infty=R=J_\infty=S_\infty=0,
   \qquad \Pi_{axis}+C_{p,\infty}=0,                \tag{2}
   \]

   so the completed pressure is the backward tail.  Neither `Pi1` nor `Pi2`
   is erased from positive-order background or canonical equations.
5. The weighted-edge theorem gives, for every fixed profile multi-index,

   \[
   |T_0|\ge c\,\zeta,\qquad
   |\partial^I T_0|+|\partial^I T_1|
      \le C_I\zeta\,\delta_{edge}^{-3|I|-3},        \tag{3}
   \]

   with a smooth `W1=T1/|T0|` at both closed edges and common compact support
   for `W_n`, `n>=2`.  This is the input used by the single coefficient
   schedule; it is not inferred from velocity bounds alone.
6. The source/target route keeps all six covariances, axial velocity, compact
   pressure, the derivative of the whole axial pressure moment and the moving
   bump.  At each finite stage it obtains the current target recurrence and
   the residual gain `B -> B+1/2-kappa`, while Gaussian cutoff tails remain a
   separate additive ledger.

Each displayed fact has an analytical provider in the table.  This checkpoint
checks that the consumer and provider are the same object family and that the
route has no dependency cycle.  It does not repeat the continuum proofs of
(1)--(3).

## 3. One common later tail, not an old first band

Every value-domain condition in the routed construction is a finite lower
bound on the integer band.  Their conjunction is therefore the maximum

\[
 \ell_F=\max\{\ell_{core},\ell_{root},\ell_{cone},\ell_{edge},
                 \ell_{coeff},\ell_{canonical},\ell_{carrier},\ldots\}.
                                                               \tag{4}
\]

All entries in (4) are fixed before the finite correction iteration and are
independent of requested derivative order.  The already selected certified
domain uses `q<qstar/64`; if an enlarged dyadic chart meets such a point, its
whole band obeys

\[
 q'\le4Q\le16q<q_\star/4.                                \tag{5}
\]

After localization the sharper ratios are

\[
 q/q_\#\le3/128,\qquad 4Q/q_\#\le3/8.                    \tag{6}
\]

The exact rational checks reproduce (5)--(6).  Equations (4)--(6) prove only
that finitely many already justified value gates share a later half-line.
They do not numerically certify an unspecified old `ell0`, identify an old
field, or remove a continuum hypothesis from one of the providers.

## 4. What is corrected, and the precise remaining frontier

The phrase “the foundation bundle remains” is too coarse in two directions.
It can be misread either as a missing scalar acceptance inequality or as a
claim that all foundations were independently certified.  The dependency
audit supports neither reading.

**Established here:** the named bundle has a complete provider route, and
that route is acyclic.  Consequently there is no additional unidentified
first-band or localization inequality between the latest explicit tail and
its listed analytical providers.  A finite maximum such as (4) handles all
of their value-domain gates.

**Not established here:** independent validity of every provider theorem.
Frozen evidence verifies source identity, exact finite algebra and the stated
routing.  A boolean in an evidence record is not a proof of a continuum
supremum, a Banach contraction, an implicit-function theorem on the selected
maps, or an all-fixed-jet edge estimate.  The remaining frontier is therefore
the following explicit conjunction, on one and the same selected profile:

1. independently validate the exact pressure-family core contraction and
   quantitative core-gap theorem;
2. independently validate continuation, matching roots, compact
   modulation/repair and annular/exterior attachment;
3. independently validate the regional cone, global pressure/moment and
   weighted first-stress edge theorems;
4. independently validate the all-fixed-jet background coefficients,
   generated-source inverse and current finite-cycle operator estimates.

Failure of any one item breaks the downstream implication at its outgoing
edge.  Success of the route checks below does not establish any of those four
continuum conjunctions.  Thus this checkpoint narrows the research task but
does not promote the conditional construction to an unconditional solution
or blow-up result.
