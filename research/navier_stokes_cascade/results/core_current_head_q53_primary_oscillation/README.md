# Q53: the primary stress forces oscillation in the final angular row

On the canonical first-real prefix, conditional on the same finite-order
physical field estimates as [Q52](../core_current_head_q52_double_viscosity/README.md),
the exponent 3−5κ cannot be increased in a uniform through-eight
auxiliary-jet estimate of the final regular angular row. The witness uses
the original primary covariance on a sequence of physical **slow**
points. The supremum is over the common auxiliary torus; it need not be
attained on the physical phase graph.

**One hole for the entire covariance.** Fix a slow point in the active
shell. By the [common atlas](../core_primary_aggregate_atlas/README.md)
there are at most 162 slow labels, signs included. Each full primary
coefficient, including the curl of the cutoff amplitude, vanishes outside
the closed enlarged rectangle for its label. In its native torus the
rectangle lies inside the projection of a square of side 8r₀:
its displacement is ξ v_r + η′ v_t with each parameter at most
2r₀ in modulus and both direction vectors of sup norm one.
The map from the common torus is a surjective integer cover and
preserves normalized Haar measure ([geometry, §§4–5](../auxiliary_geometry_audit/README.md)).
Thus the **whole preimage**, all deck copies combined, has area at most
(8r₀)². The union bound gives

    area(union) ≤ 162 (8/6057072)² < 1.

This proof does not need quantitative separation between different
copies. Finitely many closed preimages leave an open complement. There
the summed primary velocity is zero for all angular phases, hence
W = W_rθ, its complete angular covariance in Q52's normalized units,
is zero too. With Q = identity minus Haar, evaluation at any such
point gives ‖QW‖∞ ≥ |Haar W|. We never differentiate the location
of a hole or require it to be fixed across bands.

**A nonvanishing mean on shrinking bands.** Fix η=0 and any
X₀ strictly between Xrep and Xc. Section 4 of the [regional stress
floor](../regional_stress_floor/README.md) gives the component inequality
T₀θ=F(p₁−a)>F≥f*>0 there, with the state's compensated pressure and
exact functional moments. A lower bound for the norm of the two-component
stress elsewhere would not suffice. In the [original variables](../core_corrected_endpoint_join/README.md)
take z=0, q=2⁻ⁿ, t=1−q and r=√(2X₀q). Then X=X₀, η=0 exactly.
Write Q*=2^(-ℓ*) for the frozen coarsest chart, ε=Q*^h,
S=ℓ*² and s=q/Q* in [1/4,4]. Thus ℓ*=n+O(1), R stays in a compact
positive interval and the similarity weight and edge poles are fixed.

By [localized stress assembly, (3)–(4)](../core_localized_stress_assembly/README.md)
the principal physical Haar component is q^(-1-h)T₀θ. Multiplying by
the square of the common velocity normalization Q*^(2A), 2A=1+2h,
gives ε s^(-1-h) T₀θ. Both signs have already been counted in the
column solve. The only extra contribution to this component of the
original complete W is the real curl square. The [Q51 radial curl
bound](../core_current_head_q51_radial_curl_target/README.md) bounds
its modulus here by ε^(3-κ)B(S) for a fixed positive polynomial B.
This uses the original matched roots, not the later signed target.
Consequently, with b=4^(-1-h)f* independent of n,

    ‖QW‖∞ ≥ |Haar W| ≥ ε [b − ε^(2−κ) B(S)] ≥ b ε/2

for all sufficiently large n. Exponential decay in ℓ* beats every
fixed polynomial in S; the witness gives no practical onset index.

**Lower bound with the rest of the angular row retained.** Set
J₈(u)=maxᵢ ‖∂ᵢ⁸u‖∞ in the raw common torus coordinates. Q52 bounds
‖QW‖∞ by C J₈(L⁵ Nt⁻² QW) and writes the final row as that field
times ε² M⁵ j⁵ cᵢ⁻² plus a remainder. Its eight-jet bound on this
sequence is ε^(3−4κ)P(S). The multiplier is independent of the
auxiliary point; j≥j₀>0, M=cᵢ^ρ ε^(-κ) and cᵢ^(2−5ρ)≤(Tg S)³.
The reverse triangle inequality therefore gives

    J₈(Eθ) / ε^(3−5κ)
      ≥ j₀⁵ [b − ε^(2−κ) B(S)] / [C (Tg S)³] − ε^κ P(S)
      ≥ j₀⁵ b / [4 C (Tg S)³]

eventually, since ε^κ S³P(S) tends to zero. Any proposed order
3−5κ+γ, γ>0, with a fixed polynomial loss would contradict this
inequality. This is a sharp exponent up to polynomial factors within
Q52's finite-jet regime; it does not determine the minimal jet order
or the value norm. The weaker required order 3−6κ is compatible.

No cutoff, inverse, pressure gauge or support has been changed. The
bounds assume the inherited canonical fields, moment matching and the
higher jets used in Q52; the exact arithmetic controls and frozen
replay check these substitutions, not those continuum premises. Next
separate the leading terms in the **axial** row, retaining the
post-signed, pre-new-fast pressure primitive and the axial mean. The
final radial pressure compensator, evaluation on the physical graph,
uniform pulse enclosures and the full residual remain separate obligations.
