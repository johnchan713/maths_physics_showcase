Parent `7ce4bcb7694863d46aeaec1b99cf762b8ee21904` closes the
first-sourced same-state compact graph.  The existing post-signed residual
checkpoint then supplies the stronger mean state used by the older
half-line finite-stage rate recurrence, but deliberately leaves the older
first-sourced high-jet graph opaque.  This checkpoint expands that graph

The conclusion is conditional on the same all-fixed-order background,

Finite rational request arithmetic is not an evaluation of the selected

## 2. Correct interface to the older rate induction

The older conditional half-line certificate starts at

    Bold=3/2-6 kappa

and proves its affine rate inequalities for every `B>=Bold`, with gain

    delta=1/2-3 kappa>0.                                  (6)

The corrected seed satisfies

    D-Bold=4 kappa>0,       C0=D+1/2.                     (7)

Thus the rate recurrence can start at the corrected seed; no downgrade to
the old seed is needed.  This is a compatibility result for the regular
rate inequalities.  It does not by itself prove the compact inputs at later
states.

The distinction matters.  The old certificate records compact errors as
flat analytical premises but does not supply a stage-indexed backward
derivative request or common-pole/support recurrence.  A positive affine
rate margin cannot replace either of those constructions.

## 3. Exact later-stage obligation

Let

    Bj=D+j delta,   mu=1-kappa,   j>=1,                  (8)

and fix physical derivative order `m` and desired power `q^N`.  Put

    T=(N+3/2+2h+2m)/h,
    p0=max(1,floor((T-mu)/kappa)+1),
    p1j=max(1,floor((T-Bj)/kappa)+1),
    J0=m+2p0+3,       J1j=m+2p1j+3.                     (9)

The integer rule gives strict positive exponent margins.  To synchronize
the compact graph at state `j`, however, one still must prove the actual
same-state inequalities

    N_J0(Qcirc gr_j)
       <= epsilon^mu P_(j,J0)(S+K_j),                    (10)
    N_J1j(Qcirc Ez_postwave_j)
       <= epsilon^Bj Q_(j,J1j)(S+K_j),                   (11)

with a finite backward recurrence to the original primitive data and one
finite pole/operator/support ledger covering every nested call.  The desired
`A0` output may not be used earlier in that recurrence, and every `A1`
velocity must remain in later pressure and interaction terms.

Equations (10)--(11), together with their stage-indexed graph, are the
precise missing input.  The existing half-line rate arithmetic and passing
tests do not establish them for `j>=1`.  At `j=0`, equations (3)--(5) do.

Consequently this checkpoint proves conditional compact synchronization of
the corrected induction seed and compatibility with the old positive rate
recurrence.  It does not prove synchronized compact tails at every later
stage, complete physical residual decay, a controlled diagonal, admissible
forcing or blow-up.

The next obligation is to construct the finite stage-indexed recurrence for
(10)--(11), including the growing harmonic set, archived pole constants,
complete pressure memory and support extensions.  Only after that join can
the finite-stage residual powers be used in a controlled physical diagonal.
